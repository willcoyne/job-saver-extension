SettingsQuick Job Saver

Either a Google Apps Script Web App URL (Deploy → New deployment → Web app in your Apps Script project, paste the resulting /exec URL), or a local companion app URL (e.g. http://localhost:5321/api/jobs). Each person sharing this extension should point it at their own destination.

If you added a secret check in your Apps Script (so random people who find your URL can't write to your sheet), enter the same value here. It's sent with every save as a "secret" field.
